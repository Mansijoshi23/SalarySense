About SalarySense

SalarySense estimates salaries using a traditional machine learning regression model trained on a structured employee salary dataset. There is no external AI or language model involved in the prediction — every number you see comes from scikit-learn.

How the prediction is built

Data pipeline

Raw records are cleaned to remove duplicates, invalid ages, negative experience or salary values, and statistical outliers. Numeric fields such as age and experience are standardized, categorical fields such as job title and location are one-hot encoded, and skills are converted into individual indicator columns.

Salary range methodology

The displayed minimum and maximum are the prediction plus or minus the selected model's RMSE on the held-out test set. It is a transparent statistical margin based on historical model error, not a promise about any specific job offer.

Dataset

The current dataset used for training is a synthetically generated employee salary dataset built for this project, modeled on realistic relationships between experience, education, role, location, industry, and pay. It is not scraped or sourced from a live external survey, and results should be read as illustrative rather than a market benchmark.

SalarySense provides statistical estimates for informational purposes only. It does not guarantee salary outcomes and should not be treated as financial, legal, or career advice. Always cross-check estimates with current market research before making decisions.
